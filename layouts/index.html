Licensing

The thesis is operating policy. Governance and coordination systems are released under Apache 2.0; what is released stays released. End-user instruments are sold under a perpetual license — purchase buys permanent right to use; paid upgrades buy feature updates; non-payment does not revoke function. Security patches remain free for supported versions as long as reasonably manageable. Canonical specifications are published under CC BY 4.0. Brand assets are reserved.
