## What Plainsight Systems is

One company, one substrate, three proof surfaces. The substrate is **Vigil** — a custom edge-intelligence runtime that runs capable models on the operator's own hardware, with no cloud round-trip and no per-token meter. The three proof surfaces are **Type** (a keyboard-first writing instrument, in closed alpha now, with Type for Mac shipping Q3 2026), **StationZero** (home edge safety hardware, Q4 2026 POC target), and **Valet** (lifelong local personal intelligence, Q1 2027 closed-alpha target). Two operating brands run alongside — **PlainSight Lab** (governance and adversarial-resilience research) and **Appario** (AI commerce readiness for merchants) — both visible on [Portfolio](/portfolio/), neither leading the seed story.

## Why local-first matters now

Intelligence is being built as rented infrastructure: metered by the token, withdrawn at the vendor's discretion, predicated on the user surrendering data and authority. The local-first thesis is the structural refutation — intelligence that runs on the hardware the operator already owns, with no telemetry pipe, no per-token meter, and no cloud-tenancy trade. The window to build the alternative *as the alternative* — before the rented model becomes the default that everyone takes for granted — is now.

## What we are proving

The forward plan is concentrated. Type for Mac launches in Q3 2026 — the first paid software proof. StationZero's first hardware POC follows in Q4 2026 on real silicon, one safety scenario end-to-end, no cloud inference in the path. Valet's closed alpha lands Q1 2027 on macOS and Windows compute. The sequence is dependency-driven, not parallel — the Vigil x86 backend StationZero builds in Q4 unlocks Type for Windows and Valet on Windows. See [Roadmap](/roadmap/) for the full chain and the architectural reason it compounds, and [Vigil](/vigil/) for the engine that makes the sequence possible.
