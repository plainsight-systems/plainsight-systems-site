Plainsight Systems applies a single doctrine to what it publishes. Three postures for three categories of artifact.

## Open-source code — Apache 2.0

Governance, coordination, tooling, and research code released by Plainsight Systems is published under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The org's public repositories are at [github.com/plainsight-systems](https://github.com/plainsight-systems).

**Irrevocability.** Once a release has been published under Apache 2.0, Plainsight Systems does not relicense, re-close, or withdraw it. The operator's right to fork, modify, and redistribute persists regardless of subsequent company decisions, acquisitions, or dissolutions.

## Canonical specifications and written content — CC BY 4.0

Canonical specifications, invariants, and other structured written artifacts published by Plainsight Systems are released under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The operator is free to use, adapt, and redistribute these materials with attribution, for any purpose including commercial.

## Brand assets — all rights reserved

The name "Plainsight Systems", the Plainsight Systems wordmark, and any associated logomarks and visual identity elements are trademarks or service marks of Plainsight Systems LLC. They are explicitly excluded from the Apache 2.0 and CC BY 4.0 terms above. Use in derivative works, forks, or redistributions requires written permission.
