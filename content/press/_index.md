### Three paragraphs

Plainsight Systems LLC is a holding company for governance and instrument-design work across three operating brands — PlainSight Lab, Appario, and CustodyZero. The company is registered in the State of Arizona, United States, and operated by founder Andrew P Hunter, a US military veteran. Its thesis is that authority over a system should remain with the operator — before, during, and after the company's continued cooperation.

PlainSight Lab is the authority layer — it defines canonical truth, establishes invariants, and stewards correctness in domains where delayed correction produces irreversible harm. Canonical artifacts are published under CC BY 4.0. Appario is an AI commerce readiness platform for merchants, transitioning from cloud SaaS to a local-first product under perpetual license. CustodyZero is a product house for local-first instruments — applications that run on the operator's hardware, under the operator's control, with no cloud dependency. Current and near-term CustodyZero products include StationZero (home edge automation, in research), Type (writing instrument, in early testing), Valet (resident local assistant, in development), and Factory (change-control system, active and in use, Apache 2.0).

The thesis is enforced through licensing doctrine and architecture, not policy. Governance and coordination code is released under Apache 2.0 — once published, irrevocably so. End-user instruments are perpetually licensed; security patches remain free for supported versions regardless of subscription status. Local-first design, where applicable, removes the operator's dependency on the company's continued operation. The full doctrine is published at [plainsight-systems.com/licensing](/licensing/).
