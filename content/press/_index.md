### Three paragraphs

Plainsight Systems LLC is a holding company for local-first intelligence instruments, registered in the State of Arizona, United States, and operated by founder Andrew P Hunter, a US military veteran. The company is structured around three operating brands — PlainSight Lab, Appario, and CustodyZero — with three proof surfaces hosted under CustodyZero: Type, StationZero, and Valet. All three run on Vigil, the company's shared edge-intelligence runtime. The thesis: authority over a system should remain with the operator — before, during, and after the company's continued cooperation.

PlainSight Lab is the authority layer — it defines canonical truth, establishes invariants, and stewards correctness in domains where delayed correction produces irreversible harm. Canonical artifacts are published under CC BY 4.0. Appario is an AI commerce readiness platform for merchants, transitioning from cloud SaaS to a local-first product under perpetual license. CustodyZero is a product house for local-first instruments — applications that run on the operator's hardware, under the operator's control, with no cloud dependency. CustodyZero hosts three forward proof surfaces: Type (writing instrument; in closed alpha, with Type for Mac targeted for Q3 2026 launch), StationZero (home edge safety hardware; first POC targeted for Q4 2026), and Valet (lifelong local personal intelligence; closed-alpha targeted for Q1 2027). Other CustodyZero products include Factory (a change-control system for AI-assisted development, Apache 2.0, active and in use) and Note (the planned phone companion to Type).

The thesis is enforced through licensing doctrine and architecture, not policy. Governance and coordination code is released under Apache 2.0 — once published, irrevocably so. End-user instruments are perpetually licensed; security patches remain free for supported versions regardless of subscription status. Local-first design, where applicable, removes the operator's dependency on the company's continued operation. The full doctrine is published at [plainsight-systems.com/licensing](/licensing/).
