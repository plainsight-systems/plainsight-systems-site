## How the work runs

- **Start where the field started.** The tokenizer work begins with Shannon's 1948 paper, not with the latest tokenizer.
- **Build it by hand.** A library hides exactly the choke points worth studying, so the builds hand-roll what a library would provide.
- **Treat a hyperparameter as the place the theory stopped.** A number that has to be tuned is a question that has not been answered yet.
- **Credit prior art before claiming anything.** Most good ideas have been had before; the work names who had them and claims only what is left.
- **Publish what fails along with what holds.** Results go out as papers, articles or posts, whichever fits the result, and all of them are listed on this page.

The builds behind the work, and the question each one asks, are on the [Projects](/projects/) page. Code lives at [github.com/plainsight-systems](https://github.com/plainsight-systems); longer essays at [andrewphunter.com](https://andrewphunter.com/).
