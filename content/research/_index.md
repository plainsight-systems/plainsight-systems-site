## How the work runs

- **Start where the field started.** The vocabulary question begins with Shannon's 1948 paper, not with the latest tokenizer.
- **Build it by hand.** A library hides exactly the choke points worth studying, so the builds hand-roll what a library would provide.
- **Treat a hyperparameter as the place the theory stopped.** A number that has to be tuned is a question that has not been answered yet.
- **Credit prior art before claiming anything.** Most good ideas have been had before; the work names who had them and claims only what is left.
- **Publish what fails along with what holds.** Results go out as papers, articles or posts, whichever fits, and each question above links to everything it has produced.

Where the work lives: [github.com/plainsight-systems](https://github.com/plainsight-systems) for code, [andrewphunter.com](https://andrewphunter.com/) for longer essays.
